Formulas & Definitions

Cross-Sectional Area (A)
A = (π × d²) / 4
Variables & Units: d = Diameter of the cross-section (mm) | A = Cross-sectional area (mm²)
Axial Stress (σ)
σ = P / A
Variables & Units: P = Axial Force (kN, converted to N internally) | A = Cross-sectional area (mm²) | σ = Axial Stress (MPa)
Axial Strain (ε)
ε = δ / L
Variables & Units: δ = Axial Deformation (mm) | L = Original length of the member (m, converted to mm internally) | ε = Axial Strain (dimensionless / mm/mm)
Axial Deformation (δ)
δ = (P × L) / (A × E)
Variables & Units: P = Axial Force (kN) | L = Length of member (m) | A = Cross-sectional area (mm²) | E = Modulus of Elasticity (GPa) | δ = Total elongation or compression (mm)
